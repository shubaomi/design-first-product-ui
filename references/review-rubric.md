# Design Review Rubric

Review concepts, prototypes, and implementations against the approved brief. Use `block`, `revise`, or `pass`; do not hide failures inside an average score.

## Blocking failures

- Production UI was changed before the required approval gate.
- The proposed directions differ mainly by theme rather than structure.
- Essential content or actions are hidden without a reachable alternative.
- The result contradicts an explicit anti-reference or approved `DESIGN.md` rule.
- Mobile or keyboard users cannot complete the primary journey.
- Screenshots or browser evidence are missing for a material UI delivery.

## Review dimensions

### Product fit

- The first screen supports the primary job and decision.
- Visual character follows the product's purpose, not its technology category.
- Secondary workflows do not dominate the main experience.

### Structural distinction

- Layout, navigation, and interaction are intentional.
- Cards, sidebars, tables, dashboards, and modals appear only with task evidence.
- The design has one memorable device linked to the product experience.

### Hierarchy and density

- Users can identify the next action without reading explanatory paragraphs.
- Labels and microcopy clarify uncertainty rather than narrate obvious controls.
- Forms reveal fields at the right step instead of displaying configuration walls.

### Visual craft

- Typography, spacing, color, imagery, and motion form one coherent system.
- Component states and content extremes are designed, not left native by accident.
- Desktop and mobile feel composed rather than merely responsive.

### Interaction and accessibility

- Focus, keyboard order, semantics, contrast, reduced motion, and touch targets are usable.
- Empty, loading, error, destructive, and success states are honest and understandable.
- Progressive disclosure never makes essential content unreachable.

### Traceability

- Approved decisions are recorded in `DESIGN.md`.
- Deviations are visible and approved rather than silently introduced.
- Final screenshots can be compared to the approved direction.
